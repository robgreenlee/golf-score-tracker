How This App Works:

  • Click any score cell to update a player's score for that hole
  • Upload Scorecard button lets you take a photo and auto-extract scores (OCR)
  • Add Player to add new players to the group
  • Best scores only: The app only keeps your best score per hole
  • Running totals: See the sum of all recorded scores
